Matchday is a football tracker for one player's matches, training and stats,
with optional family sharing and academies. It is run by [Aarav Chopra]
("we"). Contact: [contact email].

## Without an account: nothing leaves the phone

Used without signing in, everything you enter - the player's name, date of
birth, age group, position, photo, teams, matches, results, stats, notes and
training - is kept only in this browser on this device. We never see it.
**Setup → Your data → Clear all data** removes it.

Match photos and videos are always kept only on the phone they were added on,
signed in or not.

There are no analytics, ads, trackers or session recordings in Matchday, and
its fonts are part of the app rather than fetched from Google or anyone else,
so opening it tells no third party you visited.

## The AI coach and importing fixtures

If you use the Coach tab, or import fixtures from a picture, the frames or
picture you choose and the related stats are sent to an AI service (Google's
Gemini, through Matchday's own small server) to get an answer. Matchday does
not keep them. Google's terms for its API apply to what it receives. You can
use the rest of the app without these features.

## With an account (optional)

Signing in is optional. If you do:

- **An age check comes first.** Whoever signs in is asked the month and year
  they were born. It is used there and then and never stored or sent. The
  account keeps only whether it was made by someone 13 or over, or by a parent
  or guardian who agreed to it, and when.
- **Your email address** is used only to send sign-in codes. Nobody else in the
  app sees it - family members you invite see the addresses in the household,
  and that is all. Academies and other players only ever see a **username**
  you choose.
- **The player's records** (profile including photo and date of birth, teams,
  competitions, matches with their notes, training, results, settings) are
  stored on our database so the family's phones stay in step. Everyone in the
  household can see and change them.
- **Where:** the database is hosted by Supabase in [region, e.g. London (UK)].
  Sign-in emails are sent through [Gmail / the provider used].

## Academies

An academy only sees a player's records **after the player's family says
yes**, by accepting an invite or asking to join with the academy's code. Saying
yes needs someone to confirm "I'm 13 or over, or I'm this player's parent or
guardian", and we record who confirmed and when.

Once linked, the academy's staff can **read** - never change - the player's
profile, matches with their notes, training and stats, as far as their role
allows: the owner and managers see every linked player; a coach only the
players in squads they coach; the office admin role sees no stats at all. The
academy also keeps its own records: its squads (by name), its competitions and
results, team selections, and the player's name on its list.

**Leaving** an academy (Setup → Academies → Leave) stops it seeing anything
straight away. The academy keeps the player's name on its list, marked as left.

Squad mates see each other's **names only** - never each other's stats.

Academies send us registration and safeguarding documents to be verified. Only
the academy's owner and Matchday's admins can see them.

## Who can see what, in short

| Who | Sees |
|---|---|
| Your household | Everything about the player that is synced |
| A linked academy's owner and managers | The player's records, read-only |
| A linked academy's coaches | The same, for players in squads they coach |
| An academy's office admin | Names, positions and age groups on the list - no stats |
| Squad mates | Names only |
| Matchday's admins | Academies' details and verification documents |
| Anyone else | Nothing |

Whoever runs Matchday's database can technically reach what is stored there.
We only look when needed to run the service, fix a problem, or check an
academy, and we never sell data, show ads or use tracking or analytics.

## Keeping and deleting

We keep account data while the account is in use. To have an account and
everything stored with it deleted, email [contact email] from the account's
address; we will do it within [30] days. Academies can delete themselves, their
squads, competitions and team picks from the app at any time.

## Children

Matchday is used by and for young players. Without an account it collects
nothing, at any age. Making an account is where it starts to count:

- **Signing in asks the person's age first** (month and year, not kept). Under
  13s are not let through. They are asked to get a parent or guardian, and
  picking an older year doesn't get round it: the answer stands for the rest of
  the visit. The app keeps working on the phone; it just doesn't share.
- **A parent or guardian** signs in with their own email, says they are 18 or
  over, and ticks that they agree to the child's records being kept with the
  account. The same applies when the profile on the phone shows the player is
  under 13: an adult signing in is asked to agree as their parent, and a
  teenager can't sign in with a younger child's records.
- **Academies** see a player's records only after the family ticks "I'm 13 or
  over, or I'm this player's parent or guardian", and the family can stop it at
  any time.
- A parent or guardian can ask for anything we hold about their child to be
  deleted (see Keeping and deleting). If we find an account was made for a
  child under 13 without a parent's agreement, we delete it.
