## The app

Matchday tracks a player's matches, training and stats, and optionally shares
them with family and with academies. It is free. It is provided as it is: we
work to keep it running and correct, but we cannot promise it will always be
available or free of mistakes, and stats are only as accurate as what is
entered.

**Stats are self-logged.** Players and their families enter their own matches
and numbers. Academies should treat them as the player's own record, not as
checked figures.

## Accounts

- Signing in is optional. Keep the email account you sign in with secure; a
  sign-in code is the key to your account.
- You must be 13 or over to make an account yourself. For a player under 13, a
  parent or guardian aged 18 or over makes the account with their own email and
  agrees to the child's records being kept with it. Only a parent or guardian,
  or a player of 13 or over, may agree to share with an academy.
- Answer the age question truthfully. An account made for an under 13 without
  a parent's agreement will be deleted.
- Pick a username that isn't offensive and doesn't pretend to be someone else.

## Academies

- Only set up an academy you genuinely run or are authorised to run, with its
  real name and place.
- Only invite players, and only keep names on your list, with a proper reason
  to - and respect a family's decision to say no or to leave.
- Use what you see of a player only for coaching and selecting them at your
  academy. Do not copy it out to share elsewhere.
- Staff are responsible for what they enter, including other teams' results
  and team selections.
- **The verified badge** means Matchday has checked the documents the academy
  sent. It is not an endorsement of the academy's coaching, safeguarding
  practice or quality.
- We may remove the badge, require verification, or remove an academy that
  misleads families or misuses the app.

## Don't

- Use Matchday to bully, harass or contact children in ways their family hasn't
  agreed to.
- Try to see data you haven't been given access to, or to get round the app's
  rules.
- Upload anything you don't have the right to share.

## Copyright complaints

Players and academies can upload pictures - profile photos, academy logos and
verification documents - and type in their own notes. If something on Matchday
uses your copyright work without permission, email our copyright agent at
[copyright contact email], or write to [postal address], with:

1. your name, address, phone number and email address;
2. the work you say is being copied;
3. where it is in Matchday (the academy, username or screen), in enough detail
   for us to find it;
4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised by you,
   your agent or the law;
5. a statement that what you've sent is accurate and, under penalty of perjury,
   that you own the copyright or are authorised to act for whoever does; and
6. your signature (typed is fine).

We will take the material down promptly and tell whoever uploaded it. If you
think something of yours was taken down by mistake, you can send a counter-
notice to the same address with your contact details, what was removed, a
statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake, and your
agreement to the jurisdiction of the courts [of your district, or England and
Wales if you are outside the US]. We may put it back after 10 to 14 working
days unless the complainant tells us they have gone to court. Accounts that
repeatedly upload other people's work will be closed.
