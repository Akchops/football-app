## The app

Matchday tracks a player's matches, training and stats, and can share them
with family and with academies. It is free. It is provided as it is: we
work to keep it running and correct, but we cannot promise it will always be
available or free of mistakes, and stats are only as accurate as what is
entered.

**Stats are self-logged.** Players and their families enter their own matches
and numbers. Academies should treat them as the player's own record, not as
checked figures.

## Accounts

- You sign in with your email to use Matchday. Keep that email account
  secure; a sign-in code is the key to your account.
- Nobody under 13 has an account of their own: a parent or guardian signs in
  with their own email and looks after the account. Only a parent or guardian
  can agree to share an under-13's records with an academy; a player of 13 or
  over may agree for themselves.
- Answer the age question truthfully, and don't sign in as a parent or
  guardian if you aren't one.
- Pick a username that isn't offensive and doesn't pretend to be someone else.

## Academies

- Only set up an academy you genuinely run or are authorised to run, with its
  real name and place.
- Only invite players, and only keep names on your list, with a proper reason
  to - and respect a family's decision to say no or to leave.
- Use what you see of a player only for coaching and selecting them at your
  academy. Do not copy it out to share elsewhere.
- Staff are responsible for what they enter, including other teams' results
  and team selections.
- **The verified badge** means Matchday has checked the documents the academy
  sent. It is not an endorsement of the academy's coaching, safeguarding
  practice or quality.
- We may remove the badge, require verification, or remove an academy that
  misleads families or misuses the app.

## Don't

- Use Matchday to bully, harass or contact children in ways their family hasn't
  agreed to.
- Try to see data you haven't been given access to, or to get round the app's
  rules.
- Upload anything you don't have the right to share.

## Ending

You can stop using Matchday at any time, and delete your account from the app
(Setup → Sharing with your family → Delete my account) - see the privacy
notice for what that deletes. We may suspend an account or academy that breaks
these terms.

**The coach** gives advice for a young player to try, nothing more. Its ratings
and drills are written by an AI from what it is shown; stop any drill that
hurts, and check anything that matters with a real coach.
