Privacy notice

What Matchday collects, why, who can see it, and how to withdraw your consent or delete it. Last updated [date published].

This notice is given under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it. In it, "you" is the person using Matchday (the "Data Principal"), and for a child, also their parent or lawful guardian.

The short version
  • Most of Matchday lives on your phone. With an account, the player's records are also kept on a database so your family's phones stay in step.
  • Nobody else sees them unless you invite family, or say yes to an academy. You can leave an academy at any time.
  • Match photos and videos never leave the phone they are on - unless you choose a clip for the coach to watch.
  • No adverts, no tracking, no behavioural monitoring, no selling data. Ever.
  • Anyone under 18 is a child under Indian law. A child has no account of their own: a parent or lawful guardian signs in and gives consent for them.
  • Withdraw consent and delete everything from the app - Setup → Sharing with your family → Delete my account - straight away.

3. What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is kept in this browser on this phone, so the app works with no signal. These stay only on the phone and are never sent to us: match photos and videos (except a clip you choose for the coach), the coach's conversation, any personal AI key you add, and the month and year from the age question. Matchday uses no cookies; the phone's storage is used only to make the app work.

4. Who can see it

An academy sees a player's records only after the family says yes, by accepting an invite or asking to join with the academy's code. Before that, an academy that invites a player knows only the username it typed in. For a child, only a parent or lawful guardian, signed in with their own email, can say yes.

Once linked, academy staff can read - never change - the player's profile (including photo and date of birth), matches with their notes, training and stats, as far as their role allows. The academy also keeps its own records: squads, competitions and results, team selections, and the player's name on its list. Leaving an academy (Setup → Academies → Leave) stops it seeing anything straight away; it keeps only the name on its list, marked as left.

5. Children

Under the DPDP Act, anyone under 18 is a child, and a child's personal data may be processed only with the verifiable consent of their parent or lawful guardian. Most Matchday players are children, so:

  • No child has an account of their own. Before any sign-in, Matchday asks whoever is holding the phone when they were born. Someone under 18 is asked to fetch a parent or lawful guardian, who reads this notice, confirms they are the child's parent or guardian and an adult, and signs in with their own email - the code goes to them, not the child.
  • Until a parent or guardian has signed in, a child can use Matchday on the phone only: nothing they enter leaves it, and the coach and reminders stay off.
  • Only a parent or guardian can say yes to an academy for a child. A player with no date of birth is treated as a child.
  • We do not track children, monitor their behaviour, or show them adverts of any kind, targeted or not.

6. Your consent, and withdrawing it

We process your personal data with your consent - for a child, their parent or guardian's - given when you sign in after reading this notice, and separately for each academy you agree to share with. What you send the coach, or switch reminders on for, you give us for that purpose. [Lawful bases to be confirmed by counsel.]

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and all of it is in the app:

  • Everything: Setup → Sharing with your family → Delete my account (section 8 says what that deletes).
  • An academy: Setup → Academies → Leave.
  • Reminders: Setup → Match reminders → off.
  • The coach: simply don't use it; nothing is sent until you do.

Withdrawing doesn't undo what was lawfully done before. Without an account, Matchday still works on your phone.

8. How long it is kept

We keep personal data only while it serves its purpose, and erase it when you withdraw consent or delete your account. Delete my account deletes your email address, username and age band. If nobody else is in your family - or you started it and choose "Everything" - it also deletes the player's profile, photo and every record, and any linked academy loses sight of them at once. It clears the phone too. To keep a copy first, use Setup → Your data → Export backup.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Get a summary of the personal data we hold about you or your child, what we do with it, and who it has been shared with. Export backup gives you the records themselves; Setup lists the family and academies you share with.
  • Correct, complete or update it - most of it straight from the app - or have it erased, with Delete my account or by asking us.
  • Have a grievance redressed by our Grievance Officer (section 1).
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

To use any of them, write to the Grievance Officer. We may need to confirm it is you.

10. Complaints

Write to the Grievance Officer first. We will acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Security, and breaches

We protect personal data with reasonable security safeguards. Each family's and academy's data is walled off by access rules that we test, and data travels encrypted. Reminders are encrypted so only your phone can read them, IP addresses are kept only in scrambled form, and we collect as little as each feature needs. If a breach of your personal data happens, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

12. What we never do

No adverts. No analytics or tracking - not Google Analytics, not the Meta pixel, nothing - and no cookies. No behavioural monitoring of anyone. No selling or renting data. No profiling. The coach's ratings are advice for the player, nothing more.

13. Languages, and changes

This notice is in English. You can ask the Grievance Officer for it in Hindi or any other language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India. If it changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before the change applies.
